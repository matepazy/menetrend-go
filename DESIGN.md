# Design System: MenetrendGO

Journey legs and full trip timetables request MÁV `infoServices`. Journey services occupy a full-width panel after the itinerary, headed by the route badge and selected endpoints. MenetrendGO's own pictograms (drawn at the provider's MNR2007 code points) align in a fixed leading column beside wrapping Hungarian descriptions and original validity sections, grouped into onboard facilities and ticket/pass rules; secondary operational notices expand separately. Indulások cards end with a divider and acceptance/reservation pictograms, regardless of saved passes. Tapping an icon reveals all matching rules, routes and sections. Different sections remain separate, including reservation exemptions alongside mandatory-reservation notices. Missing facilities remain unknown. The bundled pictogram font's glyphs are hidden from accessibility text and announced using service descriptions.

## Overview

Vehicle article photos open a full-screen, theme-aware viewer from either the photo or its expand control. The viewer starts on the selected photo and supports horizontal paging through that model’s gallery, with the current photo’s bundled caption as the wrapping top-left title and a page count for multiple photos. One-finger swipes page at normal size, while zoomed drags pan the image. Each page has independent zoom state, and newly visited photos retain loading and retry states. Closing preserves the article’s original carousel position. The decoded image is reused without another request. Pinch zoom is bounded to 1–5× with panning clamped to the fitted image, double-tap toggles 2.5× zoom around the tap, and an accessible zoom/reset action provides an alternative. Opening is a shared-element transform: the fitted picture grows from its exact place and rounded corners in the article into the viewer's fitted frame over 280ms, while the surface backdrop, title and zoom hint fade in. The article keeps its own photo until the viewer window has drawn its copy exactly on top, so the slot never shows empty while the window appears; only then does the motion start and the article slot clear. Closing reverses it over 220ms, easing any zoom back first; the platform dialog fade and dim are disabled so only this motion shows. After paging to another photo, arrival and dismissal fall back to opacity with gentle image scaling (220ms/160ms). All timings honour Android's animator duration scale. The overlay remains mounted through dismissal and returns to the same article and carousel position through Close or system Back.

The vehicle-model article retains the existing Read surface inside the full-height native sheet. Its curated Commons photos now download on demand at up to 960px, with a dedicated LRU cache capped at 12 photos and 12 MiB. Article text and attribution remain bundled. The image frame preserves layout while loading, names the loading state, and offers “Fotó újratöltése” after network failure without hiding the article. The original author, free licence, Commons file page and licence link remain in the source disclosure. New fleet entries preserve explicit variant matching and distinguish model capabilities from the facilities of an individual service.

**Creative North Star: "Icy blue ground, ink text, royal-blue actions, fully rounded milky panels"**

MenetrendGO feels calm, legible and direct. Its rounded character comes from solid themed panels and pale tonal separation. A feathered bottom blur softens content behind the floating phone navigation. Large rounded forms hold useful Hungarian text and familiar Material vector icons; the interface does not rely on decorative artwork.

This records the implemented Android interface. Roboto, Material controls, system bars and platform interaction anatomy are intentional. The native source is authoritative when a reference image differs. Source: `MainActivity.kt`, `PlanningScreens.kt`, `UtilityScreens.kt`, `SettingsScreen.kt`, `NearbyScreen.kt` and bundled `map.html` under `app/src/main`.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Icy blue and milky surfaces with strong ink text.
- Fully rounded panels, fields, pills and navigation.
- Native Android typography, controls and feedback.
- Text-led journey information with explicit data status.

## Colors

The primary blue draws attention to actions and selected states; cool neutrals carry most of each screen. Frontmatter colors describe the light scheme unless prefixed `dark-`.

### Primary

- **Royal Blue** (`primary`): primary buttons, meaningful icons and route lines.
- **Pale Blue Selection** (`primary-container`, `on-primary-container`): navigation item backgrounds, tonal actions and route-badge surfaces. Active phone tabs use a translucent primary-container background with primary-colored icon and label.
- **Dark-mode Sky Blue** (`dark-primary`, `dark-on-primary`): the corresponding primary pair in the dark scheme. Dark selection colors use the `dark-primary-container` pair.

### Secondary

- **Service colors**: rail categories use the shared MÁV category palette; other modes use provider route colors, with green for HÉV when its provider color is generic white.
- **Vehicle filter identities**: the filter sheet uses 40dp MenetrendGO mode pictograms, original front-view vehicle drawings generated as bundled Android vector drawables by `scripts/generate_pictograms.py`. They keep the familiar mode colors so riders still recognize them: rail navy (`#003A7E`), coaches yellow (`#FDC300`) with a dark `#3A3A39` glyph, local buses blue (`#009EE3`), HÉV green (`#005E3B`), TramTrain orange (`#F29101`), trams yellow (`#FFD800`) with a dark `#3A3A39` glyph, trolleybuses red (`#E41F18`), and replacement buses magenta (`#C4146E`) with a white train, arrow and yellow (`#FECC00`) bus. These are the mode selector pictograms, distinct from map marker/category colors. Preserve their artwork in both themes and switch states; labels and native switches communicate category and selection independently of color. Filter behavior and provider route colors remain unchanged.
- **Map Cluster Blue** (`map-cluster`): count clusters at wider geographic scales. These are map-specific roles, not additional application action colors.

### Neutral

- **Icy Ground** (`background`), **Milky Surface** (`surface`) and **Blue Mist** (`surface-variant`): the screen, solid panels and tonal controls.
- **Ink** (`ink`) and **Slate Ink** (`muted-ink`): principal information and supporting labels. `outline` is the explicit scheme outline role.
- The corresponding `dark-` tokens provide a deep blue ground, lifted blue surfaces and light text. Theme choice can follow Android or be set explicitly.
- Additional Material surface-container tones are defined by the native theme for dialogs and controls. Use the existing scheme roles rather than sampling a screenshot. Errors use Material's error role; no separate custom error palette is established here.

**The Role Pairing Rule.** Change surface and content colors together through `MaterialTheme.colorScheme`; a light-scheme ink or action label must not leak into its dark counterpart.

## Typography

**Display and body font:** Android's default Roboto through the unmodified Material 3 typography scale. There is no separate decorative display face. Sizes are Android `sp`, and spacing/radii are `dp`; the frontmatter preserves those native units.

Headlines are bold and sentence case. Headline-medium carries the planning prompt and prominent departure times; headline-small carries shared page titles, sheets and supporting time values. Title-large is used for section/card headings, with semibold or bold emphasis where the source requests it. Title-medium carries station values, action labels and key rows; selected values often use semibold. Body roles carry supporting labels, descriptions and explanatory status. Label roles carry route badges and compact navigation labels.

The frontmatter headline weights record the repeated heading treatment; individual arrival-time text can use semibold. Unspecified title/body weights retain Material defaults. Font scaling remains native, with wrapping and scrolling instead of fitting text into raster assets.

## Layout

Phone screens are single-column, vertically scrollable and inset from system bars. Content lists share `screenContentPadding()` with page padding (20dp), measured floating-navigation clearance and item gaps (16dp). `AppLayout` defines label gaps (4dp), compact gaps (8dp), related-content gaps (12dp), item gaps (16dp), page/panel insets (20dp), and section/sheet spacing (24dp). The recent-search heading uses a larger section separation, while the pass editor separates its field groups by 24dp. The map fills the available screen behind navigation without a page inset or rounded viewport. Panels and timetable cards use 20dp internal padding; compact vehicle-stop rows retain 16dp. The pass editor and vehicle drawer use sheet padding (24dp). Shared page headers have a 48dp minimum row height, 4dp between title and subtitle, and 8dp between back/title/actions, with no extra vertical padding. Timetable and vehicle rows separate time and stop columns by 12dp.

At the implemented wide-layout threshold (700dp), bottom navigation becomes a navigation rail. Main utility content is centered with a maximum width (760dp); the map can expand further (1600dp). The phone navigation surface shares the content page gutters (20dp) and departure-card corner radius (32dp), with vertical margins (6dp), inner padding (6dp) and gaps (4dp). Each item's inner content has a minimum height (48dp), growing to (64dp) above the font-scale threshold (1.15), with separate item padding (2dp horizontal, 8dp vertical). Labels may occupy two lines. Scrollable pages extend behind the floating navigation surface; its measured bottom inset is added to list content padding so final actions can scroll clear. The map retains safe control bounds. Safe drawing insets, navigation-bar padding and IME padding belong to the layout.

The planning surface opens with “Hová indulunk?” and the reminders bell on one row, followed by search and recents; there is no separate wordmark row. Results open as their own scrollable departure list. Future screens should follow the same navigation conventions.

## Elevation & Depth

The status bar is transparent. Maps and scrolling pages extend behind it, with initial list padding and map controls respecting the top safe-drawing inset. The top inset plus 24dp uses the same feathering as the bottom, mirrored vertically: an 18dp backdrop blur on Android 12+, a mask opaque through fraction .25 of the feather height, then easing to transparent along a 16-stop smootherstep curve (zero slope and curvature at both ends, so no visible knee), and a theme-background tint easing from alpha .82 to zero along the same curve over the full height. Android 9–11 use the same tonal fallback as the bottom. Native pages share one full-page blurred display list, cropped only after blurring with transparent out-of-bounds sampling to avoid stretched edge bands, and crossfaded against the sharp page so dark content is not doubled; map screens keep both edges sharp without backdrop blur, tint or feather masks across Google, railway and custom layers. System icons remain sharp. Expanded layouts retain safe rail controls; landscape display cutouts retain horizontal safe insets.

Depth comes from solid themed surfaces, tonal separation and a restrained liquid-glass material (`LiquidGlass.kt`), with no custom shadow and no translucency or blur. `GlassSurface` catches one soft highlight: a 1dp line just inside the top edge (light: white; dark: white at .09) that fades out down the sides over the upper third. There is no bottom edge, border, sheen or depth tint, so contents and selection pills stay optically centred. `Glass` panels, departure and stop-board cards, saved/recent rows, settings groups, alert cards, segmented bars, drawer summary cards, the planning date/time controls (on surface-container-low), the phone navigation and the map's floating controls all use it, as do the map-layer and vehicle-filter switch cards, departure-service stop tiles, map empty-state panels and the vehicle article's key-figure tiles. Material controls that paint their own container take the highlight alone (`glassHighlight`): tonal buttons and tonal icon buttons use the pill strength (`controlGlassTone`), secondary `GlassButton`s and unselected chips the highlight of their own container, and every filled button a softer white (.38 light, .22 dark, `buttonGlassTone`) so the primary blue stays solid; disabled controls keep 38% of their light. Dialogs, dropdown menus and the snackbar take the `Floating` highlight (the snackbar's measured on its inverse surface). Modal, expandable and map vehicle sheets and the onboarding panel carry it on their top edge (`glassEdge`, fading over 96dp), drawn from the full-width drag-handle strip because Material moves the sheet itself. Small solid marks (route and platform badges, tonal icon circles) and flat rows inside panels stay flat, so highlights never stack; floating chrome (`Floating` level) catches 1.35× the light in dark mode. Liquid selection pills carry the same top highlight over their fill (light: white at .8; dark: white at .13). While a pill stretches (2.5× its stretch ratio) or the navigation pill is held (.45, eased), the highlight brightens by up to 80%, settling with the wobble; with animations removed the pills jump and stay at rest.

Phone content extends behind the floating bar, with no fixed background strip. The bottom slice of the shared display-list blur (18dp on Android 12+) is clipped to the navbar surface top edge (the measured navigation inset minus its 6dp top margin). Its alpha mask goes from transparent at the top to opaque at fraction .55, with a background-color fade from alpha 0 to .82. Navigation controls remain sharp. Android 9–11 use the tonal fade fallback; rail layouts have no bottom veil.

The map retains its own compact marker shadows, in native canvas artwork for Google Maps and CSS for Leaflet. These do not define native Compose elevation. Dialogs, sheets, disabled states and pressed feedback continue to use Material behavior.

Motion uses the shared `AppMotion` timings: 150ms feedback, 220ms arrivals and expansion, 160ms dismissal. Screen changes use a brief opacity transition with a 6dp vertical settle, without retaining outgoing interactive screens. Phone tab selection stretches the shared liquid pill between destinations and blends item tints by coverage; the station-swap icon turns a half rotation; itinerary disclosures rotate and reveal their stops along the existing timeline. Compose respects Android's animator duration scale, including Remove animations. Station editing preserves the resting row's text and icon anchors. The focus scrim fades around a cutout expanded by the search panel's 20dp gutter, aligning it with the outer card edges; suggestions expand below the anchored row. Loading bars are `VehicleProgress`: a 24dp track (Material linear track and indicator colours) on which one of the Settings footer vehicles, picked at random per display, drives with the footer's bob, rock, speed lines and exhaust. Artwork is pre-scaled off the main thread to the bar's pixel height and fades in over 220ms. Indeterminate loads loop the pass through 16dp faded track ends, filling the track behind the vehicle and draining it as the vehicle leaves. Determinate progress fills the track to the exact value while the vehicle drives ahead of it. With animations removed the vehicle waits mid-track over an empty track. Small circular spinners inside controls stay Material. No other decorative loops or additional animation dependency are used.

All modal drawers use `AppBottomSheet`. A fixed outer top/horizontal safe-drawing inset reserves space for the status bar and display cutouts; only bottom insets remain inside the moving sheet. This keeps the expanded anchor independent of drag-driven top-inset consumption, avoiding full-height scroll/drag bouncing. Native scrolling, swipe dismissal, Back and keyboard handling remain in Material. The vehicle-model article fills the available safe height without its former 78% cap. Emulator regression coverage checks repeated flings, drag reversal frame by frame, article source disclosure and closing a nested article back to its parent drawer.

## Shapes

The update drawer caps its content at 85% of the window height, inside the shared safe-area sheet, retaining its bottom anchor and native drag handle. Long release notes scroll separately from the download, beta-confirmation and reminder actions. Release notes render as native Markdown text with themed links, headings, lists, emphasis, code and strikethrough; text follows the Material body scale and system font scaling.

The native theme's shape scale runs from softly rounded small controls to large containers, as recorded in frontmatter. Reusable panels, station rows and bottom navigation use medium corners (28dp). Result cards and dialog surfaces retain large corners (32dp); autocomplete rows use small corners (24dp). Sheet top corners retain extra-large corners (36dp); each phone navigation item has its own rounded corners (26dp). The planning date/time control uses 20dp corners. Buttons, badges and filter chips use pill geometry; circular icon controls remain circular.

**The Rounded Continuity Rule.** New controls and containers should continue the existing rounded family, including overlays and selection states.

## Components

### Buttons

The primary action is a full-width native pill with a vector icon, a title-medium label and a minimum height (58dp). Its icon is (22dp) with a label gap (10dp). Secondary actions (additional departures, date and county selection, Kérdésed van?, provider links) are `GlassButton`s: solid pills one tone above what they sit on (surface-container-high in panels and sheets, the milky surface on the page ground, surface-container-highest in the pass editor), primary-coloured content, the top glass highlight and no outline; text actions handle lighter utilities. Retain Material enabled, disabled, focus, pressed and accessibility behavior rather than inventing desktop hover choreography.

### Cards / Containers

Solid `Glass` panels group related controls or explanations, with 20dp internal padding. Departure cards place wrapping service badges at the top left, followed by equally bold departure/arrival times with station names beneath and an arrow between. Supporting date/duration text follows. Only available live data adds a status label; the scheduled-time label is omitted. Detail surfaces fill the available content width. Full stop lists share the vehicle drawer’s connected timeline and font-aware 56dp time gutter. Itineraries use a connected vertical timeline with solid provider-colored transit lines, dotted walking lines, time/stop nodes, transfer waits and expandable intermediate stops. ### Inputs / Fields

The “Keresési feltételek” drawer uses wrapping Material filter chips grouped as Utazás, Kocsiosztály and Szolgáltatások. Each chip has a bundled pictogram from the app's own font, a wrapping label, native selected semantics and color-only visual selection; touch targets remain at least 48dp. Chips have 8dp horizontal and vertical gaps and 8dp below group headings. Kocsiosztály offers 2., 1. and 1+; 1+ matches the Prémium osztály provider notice (MNR2007 190). A selection count sits below the title. Reset and Done remain pinned below the scrollable content, including at enlarged text sizes. Wi-Fi and power/USB are omitted because the current OTP source does not reliably expose evidence for them; existing service details still preserve any provider notices. Climate uses the official MÁVPlusz planner condition ESZKOZ_KERESESI_FELTETEL#10907842 on initial and paginated requests, and those results retain query provenance instead of requiring a missing climate display notice. Other conditions require positive evidence on every transit leg. Provider stop-index anchors allow wider validity sections to cover a selected subjourney; overlapping negative rules still reject it. “A szűrésről” reveals data requirements, reservation/advance-notice caveats and separate country-pass/first-class rules.

Station controls are full-width rows on the panel surface, separated by a divider and swap control. They use 4dp horizontal and 12dp vertical padding around a minimum 48dp inner height, and show the field label above the station value, with a blue functional icon and space for long Hungarian names. Tapping a station row opens a single rounded inline panel with a contained text-field label, a subtle divider and flat suggestion rows. ### Chips

Filter chips are `GlassFilterChip`s with no outline: unselected chips are solid surface-container-high pills, selected ones take the primary-container fill, and both catch the top glass highlight. Route badges carry only the compact service number (or the bundled route mark) on the provider route color, never a mode or vehicle icon beside it. ### Navigation

Four persistent destinations use icons and Hungarian labels: Tervezés, Térkép, Bérletek and Beállítások. Phone navigation is a custom native Compose row inside a solid rounded milky glass surface (see Elevation & Depth); expanded layouts use the native rail. Each equal-width item is selectable with `Role.Tab` semantics. One shared selection pill (`LiquidNavigationBar`) covers the complete icon-and-label item using primary-container alpha (.65). On selection it moves like a liquid: the edge facing the motion leads on a stiff spring and the trailing edge follows on a softer, slightly underdamped one, so the pill stretches, thins by up to 16% in height, then settles with a small wobble. The pill can be dragged horizontally. While dragged, a per-frame follower keeps it under the finger (leading edge lag 28ms, trailing edge 95ms), so it stretches with drag speed; grabbing the pill keeps the touch point fixed under the finger, and pulling past either end rubber-bands and flattens it against the bar's edge. Each destination gives a haptic tick. On release, the edges carry their velocity into the settle springs, and the pill snaps to the nearest destination (with a short flick projection) and opens it. Icon and label tint blends from on-surface-variant to primary by how much of the item the pill covers. Icons are (24dp), followed by a gap (5dp) and centered label-medium text. Back navigation uses `PredictiveBackPane`: gesture progress previews the previous screen and scales the current pane by up to .08 while translating it by up to .14 of its width away from either swipe edge. It commits on completion, resets on cancellation, and disables pane transformations when system animators are disabled. Native modal sheets retain their own back behavior. Subordinate screens also expose a visible back action. Labels remain visible and may wrap to two lines.

Holding a thumb completely still on the phone bar or rail (Android's long-press timeout, within half the drag slop) opens a small navigation menu centred just above the press point and clamped 16dp from the screen edges, with a long-press haptic. It never selects the tab under the thumb, and moving far enough to drag cancels it, so dragging the pill is unaffected. The menu is a surface-container-high glass panel (24dp corners, Material menu shadow) that scales up from its bottom edge over 220ms; it holds Szerkesztés and Kiegészítő funkciók as 48dp icon rows. Kiegészítő funkciók switches to Beállítások and opens that page directly. Szerkesztés opens the Navigációs sáv sheet: a working preview of the bar above two switches, also found under Megjelenés. Folyékony animáció off makes the pill jump to its destination and disables dragging. Feliratok az ikonok alatt off hides the labels; icons grow to 28dp and gain weight (the vector painted in a 0.7dp ring of offsets, keeping counters open), and items keep their names for screen readers.

Other single-choice controls share the same liquid selection pill (`LiquidPill.kt`): a pale-blue primary-container (.65) pill whose leading edge springs ahead and trailing edge follows (stiffness 900/damping .78 and 260/.6), so it stretches and thins in transit and settles with a small wobble. The Mentett/Legutóbbi tabs keep it locked to the swipeable panes, so a fast swipe stretches it further. Indulás/Érkezés and the pass editor's Bérlet típusa (Ország, Megye, Helyi) and Érvényesség (30 napos, Egyéni) are `LiquidSegmentedBar` controls: a milky rounded bar of equal options, tinting labels by pill coverage, with a 94% press squash and no ripple. In the pass editor sheet the bar uses surface-container-highest. Vertical radio lists (Megjelenés, Adatforrás, Frissítési csatorna, Térképtípus) draw the pill behind the selected row, flowing between rows, with the radio button kept and no ripple; Térképtípus cards are painted by the host so the pill can cross them, and their icon and summary tint by coverage. Only the phone navigation pill is draggable. With animations removed, every pill jumps directly.

### Settings

Explanations belong on the destination screens. This is a local composition within the existing Android world, with no separate palette or token scale.

## Do's and Don'ts

The vehicle-model drawer is a Read surface within the native Material sheet. Its offline article opens “Adatlap” with up to three equal key-figure tiles (Ülőhely, Állóhely, Belépés) with on-surface icons on surface-container-high with 16dp corners: a bold headline-small count only when the value parses to a plain count, otherwise the full catalog wording, and the single trailing parenthetical as a muted note. ### Do:

- **Do** use theme role pairs for every surface and its text in light and dark modes.
- **Do** retain the fully rounded family and restrained milky tonal depth.
- **Do** keep Hungarian names and labels readable through wrapping, font scaling and scrolling.
- **Do** use semantic native controls and meaningful Hungarian labels for functional vector icons.
- **Do** distinguish scheduled, live, stale, unavailable and unknown states with explicit text.

### Don't:

- **Don't** flatten interface text, controls or navigation into image assets.
- **Don't** use heavy glass blur that compromises information legibility.
- **Don't** promote mockup sample times, badges or system chrome into product facts.
- **Don't** substitute map marker styling for the native application color and elevation system.
