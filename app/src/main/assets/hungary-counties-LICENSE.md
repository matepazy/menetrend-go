# Hungarian county boundaries

Source: geoBoundaries gbOpen HUN ADM1, revision 9469f09.
https://www.geoboundaries.org/api/current/gbOpen/HUN/ADM1/

Source metadata: geoBoundaries, Wikimedia Commons; boundary year 2011;
CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication.
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Converted to county-name | lon,lat;lon,lat lines, rounded to six decimals.
The 19 county polygons include Budapest inside the Pest outline, matching
Pest pass territorial acceptance. They cannot identify Budapest-only fare exceptions.
These generalized boundaries are an estimate, not a tariff boundary authority.
The app leaves routes near unresolved boundaries uncertain. County-border stop
exceptions, temporary diversions, special trains and local acceptance outside
supported services require provider confirmation.
